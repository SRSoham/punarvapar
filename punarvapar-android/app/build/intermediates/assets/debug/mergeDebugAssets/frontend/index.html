Quick help
I cannot login

Check your phone number, password and selected role. Collector/recycler access is enabled only after verification.

My lot is not saving

Check that a material and weight are selected. The app can save locally while offline and sync later.

Camera is not opening

Allow camera permission in Android settings, then return to the New Lot screen and try again.

Data is not syncing

Reconnect to the internet and tap Sync now. Pending lots remain on the device until the server accepts them.
